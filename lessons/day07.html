Day 7: Week 1 Review & Diagnostic Speech Test

Consolidate your learning from Week 1 and set your baseline with a comprehensive diagnostic speech test.

📋 Modules

○ 1. Warm-up & Setup ○ 2. Vocabulary Review ○ 3. Grammar Review ○ 4. Speaking Fluency ○ 5. Self-Introduction Test ○ 6. Diagnostic Test ○ 7. Interview Q&A ○ 8. Error Analysis ○ 9. Writing Review ○ 10. Final Week Score
Module 1

Warm-up & Setup

Prepare your environment and mindset for a deep review and testing session.

  • Find a quiet, distraction-free space.
  • Set up your microphone and recording device.
  • Do a 2-minute vocal warm-up (lip trills, humming).
Module 2

Week 1 Vocabulary Review

Review the high-frequency words and idioms covered in Days 1-6.

  • Review your flashcards or vocabulary journal.
  • Use 5 target words in new, spontaneous sentences.
  • Identify any words you still struggle to pronounce.
Module 3

Grammar Review: Tenses

Solidify your understanding of foundational tenses used for self-introductions and daily routines.

  • Write 3 sentences in Simple Present.
  • Write 3 sentences in Present Continuous.
  • Write 3 sentences in Simple Past.
Module 4

Pronunciation Checkpoint

Focus on common challenging sounds from the past week (e.g., TH, R vs L, V vs W).

  • Record yourself reading a list of minimal pairs.
  • Compare your recording against native speaker audio.
  • Practice difficult sounds isolated and in context.
Module 5

Fluency Drill: Tongue Twisters

Improve articulation and speed with targeted tongue twisters.

  • "She sells seashells by the seashore." (Repeat 3x)
  • "Red lorry, yellow lorry." (Repeat 5x)
  • Focus on clarity over speed initially.
Module 6

Listening Comprehension Test

Evaluate your ability to understand native speakers at natural speed.

  • Listen to a 2-minute provided audio clip.
  • Answer 5 comprehension questions.
  • Identify 3 linked sounds or reductions used by the speaker.
Module 7

Reading Aloud & Intonation

Practice reading a short paragraph with appropriate stress, rhythm, and emotion.

  • Mark thought groups and stressed words on the text.
  • Read the text aloud with feeling.
  • Record and review your pacing.
Module 8

Shadowing Practice

Mimic a native speaker to absorb natural speech patterns.

  • Listen to a 30-second dialogue.
  • Shadow (repeat simultaneously) with the audio.
  • Focus on matching the exact melody and rhythm.
Module 9

Impromptu Speaking: 1-Min

Think on your feet with short, spontaneous speaking prompts.

  • Prompt: "Describe your favorite meal."
  • Speak for exactly 60 seconds without stopping.
  • Do not worry about mistakes; focus on flow.
Module 10

Diagnostic Test: Instructions

Understand the structure and goals of your baseline diagnostic speech.

  • Duration: 3 to 5 minutes.
  • Format: Audio or video recording.
  • Goal: Assess current fluency, grammar, and pronunciation.
Module 11

Test Topic Selection

Choose a topic for your diagnostic speech.

  • Option A: A significant turning point in your life.
  • Option B: Your career goals and why you chose them.
  • Option C: A detailed review of a book or movie.
Module 12

Preparation & Outlining

Take 5 minutes to outline your speech. Do NOT write a full script.

  • Introduction: Hook and thesis.
  • Body: 2-3 main points with examples.
  • Conclusion: Summary and closing thought.
Module 13

Diagnostic Speech Delivery

Record your speech. This is your baseline metric.

  • Hit record on your device.
  • Deliver your speech using only your outline.
  • Save the file as "Week1_Diagnostic_[YourName]".
Module 14

Self-Evaluation & Analysis

Listen back to your recording and objectively grade yourself.

  • Rate your fluency (1-10).
  • Rate your vocabulary usage (1-10).
  • Note the number of long pauses or "um"s.
Module 15

Identifying Weaknesses

Pinpoint the specific areas holding you back.

  • Did you struggle with finding words? (Vocab)
  • Did you use incorrect tenses? (Grammar)
  • Were some words unclear? (Pronunciation)
Module 16

Setting Goals for Week 2

Based on your diagnostic, what will you focus on next week?

  • Set 1 primary goal (e.g., "Reduce filler words").
  • Set 1 secondary goal (e.g., "Master past tense").
  • Write these goals down in your journal.
Module 17

Common Mistakes & Fixes

Review the most frequent errors made by learners at this stage.

  • Subject-Verb Agreement errors.
  • Translating directly from native language.
  • Ignoring word stress.
Module 18

Peer Feedback (Optional)

If you have a study partner, exchange recordings.

  • Provide constructive criticism.
  • Highlight one thing they did well.
  • Suggest one area for improvement.
Module 19

Building a Study Plan

Organize your schedule for the upcoming week.

  • Block out 30-60 minutes daily for practice.
  • Assign specific tasks (e.g., Mon: Vocab, Tue: Listening).
  • Commit to consistency.
Module 20

Mindset & Confidence

Address language anxiety and build speaking confidence.

  • Acknowledge that mistakes are proof of trying.
  • Visualize yourself speaking fluently.
  • Celebrate completing Week 1!
Module 21

Action Items & Wrap-up

Final checklist before you close out Day 7.

  • Save your diagnostic recording securely.
  • Review your Week 2 goals.
  • Rest and prepare for Day 8.
