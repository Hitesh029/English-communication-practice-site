DAY 22 OF 30

Executive Presence & Corporate Leadership Pitching

⏱️ 60-90 min | 📚 21 Modules | Tier: Corporate | Grammar: Persuasive rhetoric — rule of three, rhetorical questions, anaphora, power phrases

Module 1: Mindset Lesson

"Leadership is not about being in charge. It is about taking care of those in your charge." — Simon Sinek

Executive presence is a combination of confidence, poise, and authenticity that convinces the rest of us you have what it takes to lead. It is not just about what you say, but how you say it, how you stand, how you listen, and how you inspire others. Today, we will focus on persuasive rhetoric and power phrases that elevate your language from operational to visionary. When you pitch as a leader, you're not just selling a product or an idea; you're selling a future that others want to be a part of. Develop that presence today.

Module 2: Warm-up Questions

Answer the following questions to warm up.

1. What makes someone appear as a leader?

2. Who is a leader you admire and why?

3. How do you handle high-pressure questions?

4. What is the difference between management and leadership?

5. Have you ever pitched an idea to executives?

6. Why is a strong vision important for a company?

7. How do you project confidence when you are nervous?

8. What is the most persuasive speech you've heard?

9. How does body language impact executive presence?

10. What does 'leading by example' mean to you?

Module 3: Vocabulary (25 full vocab cards)

Visionary

Adj: Thinking about or planning the future with imagination or wisdom.

Strategic

Adj: Relating to the identification of long-term or overall aims and interests.

Pioneering

Adj: Involving new ideas or methods.

Impactful

Adj: Having a major impact or effect.

Transformative

Adj: Causing a marked change in someone or something.

Revolutionary

Compelling

Forward-thinking

Data-driven

Customer-centric

Results-oriented

Agile

Collaborative

Inclusive

Accountable

Module 6: Grammar & Language — Persuasive Rhetoric

Focus: Persuasive rhetoric — rule of three, rhetorical questions, anaphora, power phrases

Rule of three: 'We will build, we will grow, we will lead.' Rhetorical questions: 'Is this the future we want to build?' Anaphora: 'We believe in quality. We believe in impact. We believe in people.' Power phrases: 'The data speaks clearly...', 'Our vision is simple yet powerful...'

Module 13: HR Interview Coaching

Question: "Where do you see yourself in 10 years as a tech leader?"

Bad

I want to be a VP or CEO.

Excellent

In ten years, I envision myself driving transformative technological initiatives that not only scale the business but also empower the people building them. I aim to be a visionary leader who bridges the gap between deep technical execution and high-level strategic growth, fostering an inclusive and innovative engineering culture.
